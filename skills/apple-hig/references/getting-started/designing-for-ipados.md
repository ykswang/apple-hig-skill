# Designing for iPadOS

> Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ipados · People value iPad's power, mobility, and flexibility for media, games, detailed productivity tasks, and creative work.

## Device characteristics
- **Display** — large, high-resolution.
- **Ergonomics** — often held, but also set on a surface or stand; positioning changes viewing distance, though people are typically within about 3 feet.
- **Inputs** — Multi-Touch gestures, virtual keyboards, attached keyboard or pointing device, Apple Pencil, voice; people often combine multiple input modes.
- **App interactions** — from a few quick actions to hours of games, media, content creation, or productivity. Multiple apps open; people appreciate viewing more than one app onscreen and inter-app capabilities like drag and drop.
- **System features** — multitasking, widgets, drag and drop.

## Best practices
- **Use the large display to elevate content** — minimize modal interfaces and full-screen transitions; place onscreen controls where they're easy to reach but not in the way.
- **Use viewing distance and input mode to determine the size and density** of onscreen content.
- **Support Multi-Touch, physical keyboard/trackpad, and Apple Pencil**; consider unique interactions that combine multiple input modes.
- **Adapt seamlessly to appearance changes** — orientation, multitasking modes, Dark Mode, Dynamic Type — and transition effortlessly to running in macOS, letting people choose configurations.
