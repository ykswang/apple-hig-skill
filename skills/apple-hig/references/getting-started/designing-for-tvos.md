# Designing for tvOS

> Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-tvos · tvOS delivers vibrant content, immersive experiences, and streamlined interactions in media, games, fitness, education, and home utility apps.

## Device characteristics
- **Display** — typically very large, high-resolution TV.
- **Ergonomics** — people are many feet away from a stationary TV, often 8 feet or more, and sometimes interact while moving around the room.
- **Inputs** — remote, game controller, voice, and apps on other devices.
- **App interactions** — deep immersion in a single experience, often for hours; people also use picture-in-picture to follow another app or video simultaneously.
- **System features** — integration with the TV app, SharePlay, Top Shelf, TV provider accounts.

## Best practices
- **Support powerful, delightful interactions** through the fluid, familiar gestures of the Siri Remote.
- **Embrace the tvOS focus system** — let it gently highlight and expand items as people move among them, so they always know what to do and where they are.
- **Deliver edge-to-edge artwork, subtle fluid animations, and engaging audio** — a cinematic experience that's clear, legible, and captivating from across the room.
- **Enhance multiuser support** — make sign-in easy and infrequent, handle shared sign-in, and automatically switch profiles when the current viewer changes.
