# Designing for watchOS

> Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-watchos · People glance at Apple Watch to access essential information and perform simple, timely tasks, stationary or in motion.

## Device characteristics
- **Display** — small, wrist-sized, easy-to-read, high-resolution.
- **Ergonomics** — usually no more than a foot from the display; people raise the wrist to view and interact with the opposite hand. Always On display shows information on the watch face when the wrist is dropped.
- **Inputs** — Digital Crown (vertical navigation / data inspection; consistent across watch face, Home Screen, apps); standard gestures (tap, swipe, drag) even in motion; Action button initiates an essential action without looking; shortcuts for routine tasks; device data from GPS, blood-oxygen and heart sensors, altimeter, accelerometer, gyroscope.
- **App interactions** — many glances per day; concise interactions often under a minute each. People frequently use related experiences (complications, notifications, Siri) more than the app itself.
- **System features** — complications, notifications, Always On, watch faces.

## Best practices
- **Support quick, glanceable, single-screen interactions** that deliver critical information succinctly and enable targeted actions with a simple gesture or two.
- **Minimize navigation hierarchy depth**; use the Digital Crown for vertical navigation (scrolling or switching between screens).
- **Personalize proactively** — anticipate needs and use on-device data to provide actionable content relevant now or very soon.
- **Use complications** for relevant, potentially dynamic data and graphics on the watch face, visible on every wrist raise and tappable to dive into the app.
- **Use notifications** for timely, high-value information and to let people act without opening the app.
- **Use background content such as color** to convey supporting information; **use materials** to illustrate hierarchy and a sense of place.
- **Design the app to function independently**, complementing notifications and complications with additional details and functionality.
