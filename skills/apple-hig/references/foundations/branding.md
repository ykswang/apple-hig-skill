# Branding

> Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/branding · Apps and games express their unique brand identity in ways that make them instantly recognizable while feeling at home on the platform and giving people a consistent experience.

## When to use / core idea
- Express brand in the app icon, throughout the experience, and on the App Store (see App Store Marketing Guidelines).
- Brand should be refined and unobtrusive: content first, familiar components and patterns, restrained color and logo use.

## Rules
### Best practices
- **Use your brand's unique voice and tone in all the written communication you display** — e.g., encouragement/optimism via plain words, occasional exclamation marks and emoji, simple sentences.
- **Apply your app's accent color judiciously** — broad use overwhelms the interface and dilutes impact. Minimize it on controls; use it intentionally for primary actions or status indicators (unread badges, selected tab icon in a tab bar). To express brand through color, consider moving it into the content layer, where it scrolls beneath Liquid Glass controls and is picked up dynamically.
- **Consider using a custom font** — if strongly brand-associated, ensure legibility at all sizes and support for bold text and Dynamic Type. Works well: custom font for headlines/subheadings, system fonts for body copy and captions (optimized for small sizes).
- **Express your brand with familiar components** — if customizing a component's appearance, preserve platform-appropriate sizing, placement, and behavior.
- **Ensure branding always defers to content** — don't spend screen space on elements that only display a brand asset.
- **Help people feel comfortable by using standard patterns consistently** — UI in expected locations, standard symbols for common actions, established navigation and modality conventions.
- **Resist the temptation to display your logo throughout your app or game unless it's essential for providing context** — people seldom need reminding which app they're in.
- **Avoid using a launch screen as a branding opportunity** — it disappears too quickly; consider a welcome/onboarding screen with branding at the start instead.
- **Follow Apple's trademark guidelines** — Apple trademarks must not appear in your app name or images.

## Platform considerations
No additional considerations: iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, watchOS.
